# concept-rocket

Reference: art/selected/style-frame.png (except the initial style frame).

Square modeling concept sheet for Rocket, energetic adult female pickleball power player, clay-orange polo, cream shorts, cream sport visor, tied dark curly hair, brown skin, chunky rounded adult proportions. Three full-body views front, three-quarter, back, and three facial expressions focused, pleased, rueful. Plain cream paper, no text or labels.

Match docs/art-bible.md: rounded matte forms, upper-left soft key, palette of evergreen/sage/cream/clay/violet, restrained grain. Reject stray text, warped faces, extra fingers, inconsistent style. Four independent variants, explicit review before selection.
