# concept-june

Reference: art/selected/style-frame.png (except the initial style frame).

Square modeling concept sheet for June, confident adult female club pickleball champion, evergreen polo, cream shorts, cream headband, short dark hair, tan skin, chunky rounded adult proportions. Three full-body views front, three-quarter, back, and three facial expressions focused, quietly pleased, shrugging. Plain cream paper, no text or labels.

Match docs/art-bible.md: rounded matte forms, upper-left soft key, palette of evergreen/sage/cream/clay/violet, restrained grain. Reject stray text, warped faces, extra fingers, inconsistent style. Four independent variants, explicit review before selection.
