# style-frame

Reference: art/selected/style-frame.png (except the initial style frame).

Square style frame of a complete neighborhood pickleball court in a wood-and-clay toy diorama at golden hour. Two grounded adult players, cap silhouettes, soft rounded trees, benches, green court and kitchen lines. Whole court readable in a three-quarter view.

Match docs/art-bible.md: rounded matte forms, upper-left soft key, palette of evergreen/sage/cream/clay/violet, restrained grain. Reject stray text, warped faces, extra fingers, inconsistent style. Four independent variants, explicit review before selection.
