# app-icon

Reference: art/selected/style-frame.png (except the initial style frame).

Square app icon on transparent background: a chunky rounded evergreen pickleball paddle with a cream stripe, clay handle and a small cream perforated pickleball, simple warm hand-cut wood-and-clay diorama styling, strong silhouette readable at 64 pixels, centered with transparent padding, no letters, logos, border or cast shadow.

Match docs/art-bible.md: rounded matte forms, upper-left soft key, palette of evergreen/sage/cream/clay/violet, restrained grain. Reject stray text, warped faces, extra fingers, inconsistent style. Four independent variants, explicit review before selection.
