# texture-atlas

Reference: art/selected/style-frame.png (except the initial style frame).

Square seamless texture reference atlas with exactly four equal square tiles in a 2 by 2 grid, no gaps or borders: top-left clay pavement grain, top-right sage painted sports court with subtle worn cream paint flecks, bottom-left short sage grass, bottom-right warm bench wood grain. Completely flat orthographic materials, even light, no objects, shadows, letters or perspective. Each individual tile should seamlessly repeat horizontally and vertically.

Match docs/art-bible.md: rounded matte forms, upper-left soft key, palette of evergreen/sage/cream/clay/violet, restrained grain. Reject stray text, warped faces, extra fingers, inconsistent style. Four independent variants, explicit review before selection.
