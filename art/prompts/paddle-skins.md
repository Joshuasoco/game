# paddle-skins

Reference: art/selected/style-frame.png (except the initial style frame).

Square transparent sprite sheet of six separate pickleball paddles, flat orthographic top-down with handles pointing down, exactly three equal columns and two equal rows, generous transparent gaps, no perspective or shadow. Designs row order: evergreen stripe; clay sun; sage checker; violet arch; cream court diagram; evergreen champion band. Matte cream edges, wrapped clay handles, bold simple readable motifs. No text or logos.

Match docs/art-bible.md: rounded matte forms, upper-left soft key, palette of evergreen/sage/cream/clay/violet, restrained grain. Reject stray text, warped faces, extra fingers, inconsistent style. Four independent variants, explicit review before selection.
