# concept-pip

Reference: art/selected/style-frame.png (except the initial style frame).

Square modeling concept sheet for Pip, friendly balanced adult pickleball player, sage polo, cream shorts, cream baseball cap, short dark hair, medium warm skin, chunky rounded adult proportions. Three full-body views front, three-quarter, back, and three small facial expressions ready, quietly pleased, disappointed. Plain cream paper, no text or labels.

Match docs/art-bible.md: rounded matte forms, upper-left soft key, palette of evergreen/sage/cream/clay/violet, restrained grain. Reject stray text, warped faces, extra fingers, inconsistent style. Four independent variants, explicit review before selection.
