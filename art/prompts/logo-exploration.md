# logo-exploration

Reference: art/selected/style-frame.png (except the initial style frame).

Square logo exploration sheet, four distinct understated wordmark directions for the exact lowercase text dinkduel, each paired with a simple rounded paddle-and-ball club mark. Evergreen ink on cream paper, a small clay dot accent. Crafted confident sporty typography, no extra taglines or text, no neon or gloss. Four equal quadrants, each a coherent design option.

Match docs/art-bible.md: rounded matte forms, upper-left soft key, palette of evergreen/sage/cream/clay/violet, restrained grain. Reject stray text, warped faces, extra fingers, inconsistent style. Four independent variants, explicit review before selection.
