# Dink Duel voice

Short. Dry. Warm. Say what happened, then what to do.

- One title line: “Small court. Big energy.” No other slogans.
- Use “Your serve.”, “Let it bounce.”, “Fault: kitchen volley.”, “Phone disconnected. Reconnecting…”
- Buttons name actions: “Pair phone”, “Rematch”, “Resume game”, “Use touch controls”.
- Rival quips are one understated sentence. Never taunt a beginner.
- Avoid exclamation marks, all-caps instructions, corporate reassurance and commentary on every hit.
- Distinguish an input from a hit: the phone acknowledges a swing immediately; confirmed contact has a separate haptic.
- Keep existing accessible action names where they are part of the tested control contract.
