# Real-phone playtest — not yet measured

Record phone model, OS/browser version, laptop/GPU, quality setting, connection type, date, and observer. Use a secure hosted/tunnel URL for motion. A browser emulation pass does not complete this checklist.

1. **Pair from a cold start.** On iPhone Safari and Android Chrome, scan a new room and complete three forehand practice swings. Time QR scan to first legal hit; target under 60 seconds. Confirm a second phone cannot take the room.
2. **Deny motion permission, then use HTTP.** Confirm both cases offer working touch play without repeated permission prompts. Let the phone idle for two minutes and verify wake lock behavior; lock/unlock once and check reacquisition.
3. **Replay a deliberate sensor sample set.** Record at least 20 gentle and 20 strong swings plus one minute of ordinary handling on each phone. Count hits, misses and return-motion double triggers. Detection target is at least 95%; handling false positives below 5%. Keep raw samples outside public assets and obtain consent before sharing.
4. **Aim with both hands and orientations.** Recalibrate right/left grip, aim at left/center/right, then rotate portrait/landscape. Check neutral drift and mirror placement. Document device-specific axis changes; do not infer sensor correctness from the landscape CSS check.
5. **Measure response and timing.** Film the phone and screen together at high frame rate for 20 swings. Compare swing peak, local flash and visible contact; target under 100 ms on local Wi-Fi. Log RTT separately. Verify Perfect/Good/Late responses feel connected to contact timing, not packet arrival.
6. **Exercise all five shots.** Land a soft dink, fast drive, kitchen drop, deep lob and high-ball smash. Deliberately mistime and overhit drives. Confirm misses have visible causes, power changes pace, and spin differences are noticeable without dominating the rally.
7. **Use thumb controls while holding the phone safely.** Hold LOB, toggle DROP, hold advance/retreat and pause in both hands. Check for accidental triggers, lost holds after pointer cancel, scrolling or grip strain. Use a strap or firm grip and clear the surrounding space.
8. **Interrupt Wi-Fi mid-rally.** Disable networking for three seconds. Confirm pause, reconnect, unchanged score, unchanged serve and resumed control; target recovery within five seconds after Wi-Fi returns. Repeat after a page reload and after the 60-second reservation expires.
9. **Play rivals and streaks.** Finish matches against Pip, Rocket, Mabel and June. Check Rocket makes pressure mistakes, Mabel struggles with deep lobs, and June varies placement. Play three wins/losses in a row; the small adaptation should remain unobtrusive and must not alter swing detection.
10. **Run a five-minute quality/accessibility pass.** On an integrated-GPU laptop compare Low/Medium/High while driving and lobbing; log FPS and frame time (60 FPS laptop / 30+ FPS controller targets). Check ball visibility, net/floor tunneling, sound/volume/mute persistence, reduced motion, keyboard focus and controls. Keep local tuning counters off until the tester opts in; confirm no analytics network traffic.

Hosted HTTPS deployment, iOS/Android accuracy, physical Wi-Fi response and real hardware performance remain open gates. The API pipeline has not been exercised against a user account without `OPENAI_API_KEY`; the included art was produced with the built-in image tool and the underlying model is not exposed.
